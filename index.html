ABOUT OSHI

Organization for Strategic Health Interventions (OSHI) is local NGO established under nongovernmental organization act No 24 of 2002 with registration no ooNGO/R/1179. OSHI works in partnership with the Ministry of Health, President’s Office – Regional Administration and Local Government (PO-RALG), Ministry of Water, Ministry of Education Science and Technology( MoEST) and private sector, with the objective of facilitating the provision of high-quality health services at the community level, supporting community initiatives in provision of social services i.e. health education, health, Water Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH), promoting awareness, access, acceptance, and uptake of health services, strengthening health care systems and institutions at various levels as well as promoting environmental conservation and protection.

Helping Children Overcome Childhood Blindness

Laurent Mwashiuya was three years old when he was found to have a congenital cataract in his right eye. Through OSHI's eye-health screening and referral work with partner hospitals, Laurent received surgery and is now recovering well.

  • The challenge

    A congenital cataract in Laurent's right eye was affecting his sight from early childhood.

  • The intervention

    OSHI's community screening identified the case and supported referral for surgical treatment.

  • The outcome

    Laurent underwent successful surgery and was reported happy and recovering afterward.

Environment & Climate Action

Help Tanzania fight deforestation

Forests are vital for life on Earth — supporting biodiversity, regulating climate, and sustaining millions of livelihoods. In Tanzania, deforestation threatens ecosystems, communities and the planet.

OSHI promotes community-led forest conservation, sustainable land management, biodiversity protection and climate-smart livelihoods to restore forests and build resilient communities.
