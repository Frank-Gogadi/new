About OSHI

Who We Are

A local Non Government Organization

Organization for Strategic Health Interventions (OSHI) is local NGO established under nongovernmental organization act No 24 of 2002 with registration no ooNGO/R/1179. OSHI works in partnership with the Ministry of Health, President’s Office – Regional Administration and Local Government (PO-RALG), Ministry of Water, Ministry of Education Science and Technology( MoEST) and private sector, with the objective of facilitating the provision of high-quality health services at the community level, supporting community initiatives in provision of social services i.e. health education, health, Water Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH), promoting awareness, access, acceptance, and uptake of health services, strengthening health care systems and institutions at various levels as well as promoting environmental conservation and protection.

🎯

Our Mission

"Assist communities in poverty reduction, accessing social and environmental management services through systems strengthening."

🔮

Our Vision

A community free of poverty, diseases and environmental destruction."

Our Object

Our Strategic Objectives

01

Community Health Services

To facilitate the provision of high-quality health services at the community level.

02

Health Awareness & Access

To promote awareness, access, acceptance and uptake of health services including eyecare, HIV, FP/Reproductive health, TB, Malaria etc. at the community level.

03

Health Systems Strengthening

To strengthen health care systems and institutions at various levels.

04

Environmental Conservation & Protection
