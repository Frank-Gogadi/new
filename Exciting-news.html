News / Eye Health

We are delighted to share some wonderful news! OSHI has been awarded a £15,000 grant from the Ulverscroft Foundation in support of our ongoing project to prevent and control childhood blindness.

This project is currently being implemented in Tunduma Town Council, Songwe Region, where we are working closely with local health authorities and communities to identify children with severe visual impairment, provide timely treatment, and ensure they receive the care they need to thrive.

Importantly, this new grant from the Ulverscroft Foundation complements the initial grant of $10,000 provided by the Seva Foundation, further strengthening our capacity to expand our reach and impact in safeguarding children’s sight and future.

We extend our deepest gratitude to the Ulverscroft Foundation trustees and the Seva Foundation for their trust and support. With this combined support, we are even more determined to continue advancing our mission in the region.
